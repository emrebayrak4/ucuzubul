## Why visit San Diego

San Diego is known for sunny, mild weather, a laid-back beach culture and some of California's most family-friendly attractions. Within a short drive you can visit world-famous zoos, wander a huge park filled with museums, watch sea lions on rocky coves and eat excellent Baja-style tacos. The city is also a craft beer hub and sits just north of the Mexican border.

## Top sights and experiences

- **Balboa Park** — gardens, Spanish Revival architecture and more than a dozen museums.
- **San Diego Zoo** — a renowned zoo set inside Balboa Park.
- **La Jolla Cove** — sea lions, seals, tide pools and kayaking along the coast.
- **USS Midway Museum** — a former aircraft carrier turned floating museum.
- **Coronado Beach** — wide, sparkling sand in front of a landmark Victorian hotel.
- **Old Town San Diego State Historic Park** — early California history, shops and Mexican food.
- **Torrey Pines State Natural Reserve** — clifftop trails above the Pacific.
- **Cabrillo National Monument** — harbor views, a historic lighthouse and seasonal whale watching.
- **Gaslamp Quarter** — Victorian-era buildings packed with restaurants and bars.
- **San Diego Zoo Safari Park** — open-range habitats in the hills north of the city.

## Where to stay: best neighborhoods

**Gaslamp Quarter** suits nightlife lovers and convention-goers. Pros: walkable dining and bars, near Petco Park. Cons: noisy on weekend nights.

**Little Italy** works for food lovers and couples. Pros: excellent restaurants, a weekly farmers market, close to the airport. Cons: some hotels can be pricey.

**La Jolla** fits travelers seeking upscale coastal scenery. Pros: beautiful coves, boutiques. Cons: far from downtown and higher rates.

**Pacific Beach** appeals to younger travelers. Pros: beachfront boardwalk, casual bars. Cons: busy and loud, especially in summer.

**Coronado** is good for families. Pros: calm streets and a huge beach. Cons: separated from downtown by a bridge or ferry.

**Mission Valley** suits budget-minded visitors. Pros: many mid-range hotels, trolley access, central freeway location. Cons: not much character.

## Things to know before you go

- Mornings near the coast can be overcast in late spring and early summer.
- Tipping follows US norms, roughly 15 to 20 percent at restaurants.
- International visitors should check official sources for US entry requirements; domestic flyers should confirm their ID meets TSA rules.
- If you plan to cross into Tijuana, check official sources for passport and re-entry requirements and confirm your rental car and insurance allow it.
- Check official government travel advisories before your trip, and lock valuables out of sight at beach parking lots.
- Respect posted closures around seal and sea lion areas, and keep a safe distance.
- Pedicab drivers may quote prices per person rather than per ride; agree on the total first.

## Getting around

The San Diego Trolley links downtown with Old Town, Mission Valley, the UC San Diego area and the US–Mexico border at San Ysidro. Buses reach the beaches, and ferries cross to Coronado. The airport is close to downtown, with a shuttle to the Old Town Transit Center. Many visitors rent a car for flexibility, especially for La Jolla and North County.

## Best time to visit

San Diego is pleasant year-round. Spring and fall bring warm days and fewer crowds. Summer is the busiest season, while winter is mild and quieter, with gray whale migration along the coast.

## Budget tips

- Visit outside summer and school holidays for lower hotel rates.
- Stay in Mission Valley or near a trolley stop and save on parking.
- Enjoy free beaches, the Balboa Park grounds and coastal trails.
- Check Balboa Park's resident and visitor programs and combination passes for museums.
- Eat at taco shops for affordable, local favorites.
- Compare multi-attraction passes if you plan to visit the zoo and other ticketed sights.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many days do I need in San Diego?

Three to four days covers Balboa Park, the zoo, a couple of beaches and La Jolla. Add time for the Safari Park or a day trip.

### Is San Diego good for families?

Yes. Zoos, beaches, parks and kid-friendly museums make it one of the most family-friendly cities in the US.

### Can I visit Mexico from San Diego?

Yes, many visitors cross into Tijuana. Bring the proper documents and check official sources for current border requirements.

### Do I need a car in San Diego?

It is helpful but not essential if you stay downtown and use the trolley, buses and ride-hailing.
